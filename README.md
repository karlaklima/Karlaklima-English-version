🚀 About me

My path across industries — fintech, manufacturing, electric mobility, and healthcare — wasn't planned, but it shaped the way I think about data. Working across different business contexts gave me a broader view of how data problems vary and what it actually takes to solve them.

Today, as a Senior Analytics Engineer, I focus on building data solutions that are technically sound and directly connected to business outcomes: reducing cloud costs, improving data reliability, and enabling teams to make better decisions faster.

I'm driven by the full cycle — the technical challenge, the business problem behind it, and seeing the impact of what I build.
